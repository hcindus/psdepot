📌 What Is a Resale Certificate?

A resale certificate (also called a seller's permit, reseller license, or sales tax exemption certificate) allows your business to purchase goods tax-free when those goods will be resold to customers. Each state issues its own certificate. Simply submit your valid certificate to us and we'll keep it on file for all future tax-exempt orders.
