California-Specific Requirements

Prop 65 Compliance

California's Proposition 65 requires warnings on products containing chemicals known to cause harm. Many thermal papers contain BPA, which requires labeling. Our phenol-free options eliminate this concern entirely.

Recycling Laws

California has aggressive recycling targets. Traditional BPA receipts contaminate recycling and must be trashed. Phenol-free receipts can be recycled with regular paper—helping your business meet sustainability goals.
