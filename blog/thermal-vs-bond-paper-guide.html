Thermal vs Bond Paper — Which Does Your POS Printer Need?

Published August 5, 2026 · 5 min read · By Performance Supply Depot

Buying the wrong paper for your receipt printer isn't just wasteful — it can damage your printer. Thermal printers only accept thermal paper, and impact printers only accept bond paper. Using the wrong type can destroy a printhead in minutes. Here's how to know which one you need.

🖨️ Thermal Paper

How it works: Heat-sensitive coating creates images when heated by the printhead. No ink, no ribbon.

Used by: Epson TM-T88, Star TSP100, Clover Station, Square Terminal, Toast POS

Cost: $0.02–0.05 per receipt

Durability: 5-10 years (phenol-free: 10+ years)

Best for: Retail, restaurants, cafes, bars — any modern POS system

📄 Bond Paper

How it works: Physical impact from pins strikes an ink ribbon onto paper. Requires replacement ribbons.

Used by: Epson TM-U220, Star SP700, older kitchen printers, dot-matrix impact printers

Cost: $0.01–0.03 per receipt + ribbon costs ($8-15/ribbon)

Durability: 20+ years (physical ink, no fading)

Best for: Kitchen environments (heat-resistant), carbon copy needs

How to Tell Which Printer You Have

Look at the printer label. Find the model number (usually on the bottom or back). Search "[model number] paper type" online. Or call us at (888) 881-6834 — we'll identify it in 30 seconds.

The "scratch test": Rub a fingernail quickly across your existing receipt paper. If a dark mark appears, it's thermal. If nothing happens, it's bond.

Why Using the Wrong Paper Is Dangerous

  • Thermal paper in an impact printer: Won't print — impact pins need friction from bond paper to transfer ink
  • Bond paper in a thermal printer: Can scratch and permanently damage the ceramic printhead ($150-400 replacement)
  • Label/adhesive paper in either: Adhesive residue gums up rollers and cutters, causing jams
