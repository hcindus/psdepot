Best POS Paper for Restaurants in 2024

By Performance Supply Depot | January 2024 | 5 min read

Choosing the wrong receipt paper costs California restaurants time and money. This guide covers: which paper size fits your printer, why BPA-free matters for health, how to prevent jams during rush hour, and our top pick for busy kitchens.

Why Restaurant Receipt Paper Matters More Than You Think

In a busy restaurant, receipt paper is one of those things you don't think about—until it causes problems. Cheap paper jams during Friday night rush. The wrong size doesn't fit your printer. And BPA-coated receipts? That's a health concern for staff handling hundreds of receipts daily.

Here's what actually happens when you pick the wrong paper:

  • Printer jams during peak hours = angry customers
  • Faded receipts after 3 months = tax audit problems
  • Wrong width = wasted money on returns
  • Cheap coating = employees exposed to chemicals

The Two Sizes: Which One Fits Your Printer?

California restaurants use one of two standard widths. Here's how to tell which you need:

2¼" (58mm) - Credit Card Machines & Small Printers

Found in: Clover Mini, Square Terminal, mobile printers. Best for: Compact spaces, food trucks, counter service.

3⅛" (80mm) - Full-Size POS Printers

Found in: Epson TM-T88, Star TSP100, most kitchen printers. Best for: Full-service restaurants, kitchens, businesses that print itemized receipts.

Quick Check: Which Size Do I Have?

Look at your current roll. If it's about the width of a credit card = 2¼". If it's wider than a deck of cards = 3⅛". Still unsure? Our team can identify it from a photo—call (888) 881-6834.

BPA vs. BPA-Free: What Restaurant Owners Need to Know

Thermal paper is coated with chemicals that react to heat. Traditional paper uses BPA (Bisphenol A) or BPS. Here's why California restaurants are switching:

  • Health: BPA transfers through skin contact. Cashiers handle 100+ receipts daily.
  • Recycling: BPA paper can't be recycled. BPA-free goes in regular paper recycling.
  • Customer perception: More customers ask about "chemical-free receipts."

Preventing Jams: The Restaurant Owner's Guide

Paper jams always happen at the worst time—Friday 7 PM with a line out the door. Here's how to prevent them:

  1. Buy quality paper. Cheap paper has inconsistent coating that flakes off inside printers.
  2. Check the core size. 1" core won't fit in 3/8" printers. Verify before ordering.
  3. Store paper right. Heat and humidity affect coating. Keep unopened boxes in back room, not above ovens.
  4. Clean monthly. Use printer cleaning cards to remove debris from rollers.
