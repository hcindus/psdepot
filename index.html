Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about POS supplies and services

What is phenol-free thermal receipt paper?

Phenol-free thermal paper is formulated without phenol-based developers like BPA or BPS. We stock Clarion™ by Domtar—the first phenol-free POS paper certified recyclable in North America. It performs identically to standard thermal paper: bright white, 10-year image life, and backside printable for branding or compliance messaging.

What size receipt paper do I need for my POS printer?

Most receipt printers use either 2 1/4" (58mm) or 3 1/8" (80mm) width paper. Not sure which one fits your printer? Check your current roll's width, look at your printer's model number, or just give us a call at (888) 881-6834. We carry all standard sizes including custom lengths for specific POS systems like Square, Clover, and Toast.

POS printer repair near me—do you service California?

Yes! We repair Epson, Star, and all major POS printer brands throughout California. Our technicians cover the Bay Area, Los Angeles, and San Diego regions. In-shop diagnostics run $195/hour with most repairs completed same-day. On-site service available for $180/hour with emergency repairs for restaurants and retail businesses that can't afford downtime.

Best POS hardware for small businesses—what do you recommend?

For small businesses, we typically recommend the SAM4S ER-260 series cash registers starting at $495—reliable, programmable, and no monthly software fees. For growing businesses, we carry Samsung POS terminals and integrated scales. Every recommendation is based on your transaction volume, industry, and budget. Call us for a free consultation.
