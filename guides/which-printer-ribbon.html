Which Printer Ribbon Do I Need?

Impact printer ribbons aren't universal. Epson, Star, and other brands each use specific cartridge types. Get the wrong one and it won't fit — or worse, it fits but prints garbage. Here's the definitive compatibility guide.

How to Identify Your Ribbon Type

  1. Look at the old ribbon cartridge. The type is printed on the plastic housing — "ERC-38" or "ERC-30/34/38" is most common.
  2. Check your printer model. If the cartridge is missing or unreadable, the printer model tells you which ribbon to buy. The model number is on a label on the bottom or back of the printer.
  3. Count the pins. 9-pin printers use one ribbon family, 24-pin use another. Kitchen printers are almost always 9-pin.

The Most Common Ribbon: ERC 30/34/38 (SKU 62245)

If you have a standard kitchen impact printer — Epson TM-U220, Star SP200, or similar — you almost certainly need the ERC 30/34/38 ribbon (SKU 62245). This is the single most common POS printer ribbon in North American restaurants. It prints in black, fits dozens of printer models, and produces crisp, dark impressions that survive grease, heat, and humidity.

Impact vs Thermal: Do You Even Need Ribbons?

Ribbons are only for impact printers — those old-school printers that physically strike paper through an ink ribbon. If your printer uses thermal paper (smooth, heat-sensitive rolls), it doesn't use ribbons at all — you just need the right thermal paper. If you're not sure which type you have, check our thermal vs bond paper guide.

When to Replace Your Ribbon

Replace ribbons when prints become faint or patchy — usually every 2-4 million characters, which translates to roughly 2-4 weeks in a busy kitchen. Don't wait until prints are illegible — by then you've been sending hard-to-read tickets to the kitchen for days. Keep a spare ribbon on hand and change it on schedule, not when it fails.
