Pro Tips for New Businesses

  1. Order before you open. Running out of receipt paper on day 3 is a bad look. Over-order your first month and dial back once you know your burn rate.
  2. Verify printer compatibility. Check our printer compatibility guide before ordering paper. Paper that's 1/8" too wide won't fit.
  3. Set up autoship. Once you know your monthly usage, we can set up recurring orders so you never run out. Call (888) 881-6834 to set it up.
  4. Training matters. New staff over-pour, over-print, and over-use supplies. Budget 20% more supplies for your first month and train habits early.
  5. Bulk ordering saves 15-25%. If you're opening multiple locations, order centrally for volume discounts.
