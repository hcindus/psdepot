About Performance Supply Depot

California's trusted source for POS supplies since 2005. Family-owned, customer-focused, and relentlessly reliable.

Our Story

Performance Supply Depot LLC started in 2005 with a simple idea: businesses shouldn't have to overpay for the supplies that keep their checkout counters running. Thermal paper, receipt rolls, cash drawers, printer ribbons — these aren't luxuries. They're the backbone of every transaction.

For two decades, we've supplied restaurants, retail stores, hotels, medical offices, and businesses of every size across California and nationwide. We've watched POS technology evolve from dot-matrix impact printers to cloud-connected tablets. Through every change, one thing stayed constant: businesses need a reliable supplier who answers the phone and ships on time.

What We Do

We stock and ship POS consumables and hardware:

  • Thermal paper rolls (3 1/8" and 2 1/4")
  • Credit card terminal paper
  • Bond paper and carbonless paper for impact printers
  • Printer ribbons (ERC 30/34/38, Star SP700, and more)
  • Cash drawers and till inserts
  • Label printing scales (CAS series)
  • SAM4S cash registers and POS terminals
  • Capton beverage pouring systems
  • Barcode labels and shipping labels
