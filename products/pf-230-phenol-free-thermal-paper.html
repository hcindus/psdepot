The First Certified Recyclable Phenol-Free Thermal Paper

PF-230 Clarion™ by Domtar is the first phenol-free thermal receipt paper certified recyclable in North America per WMU standard. It looks and performs the same as standard POS paper, making it the clear choice for businesses that care about environmental responsibility without sacrificing quality.

Phenol-Free Formulation

  • 100% Phenol-Free: Formulated with a developer system free of phenols
  • No Harmful Chemicals: BPA, BPS, and phenol-free — made without Pergafast™ 201
  • Health Conscious: Safer for employees and customers handling receipts daily
  • Environmental Leader: First phenol-free paper certified recyclable across all paper grades

Same Great Performance

  • Bright White: Matches the performance and bright, white shade of standard POS papers
  • Backside Printable: White on back and front — perfect for adding brand messaging, advertising, or environmental compliance messaging
  • Flexographic Compatible: Works with flexographic printing for both front and back
  • Long-Lasting: Printed image lasts 10 years; 3 years storage shelf life under proper conditions

Certified Recyclable

  • WMU Certified: First phenol-free POS paper certified as recyclable in North America
  • Easy Recycling: Recycle Clarion™ receipts with your other paper products
  • All Grades: Recyclable across all paper grades — no special handling required

Made in the USA

  • Domestic Manufacturing: Paper manufactured in Bennettsville, SC
  • Quality Coating: Coated at Domtar's West Carrollton, OH facility
  • Reliable Supply: Get products when and where you need them from a trusted domestic partner
  • Sustainable: Made with domestic and globally sourced materials

Compatible With

Most thermal receipt printers including Epson TM-T88, Star TSP100, Clover, Square, Toast, and all standard 3 1/8 inch thermal printers. Ideal for retail stores, restaurants, grocery stores, and any business issuing receipts.
