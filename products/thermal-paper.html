What Size Thermal Paper Do I Need?

Most receipt printers use either 2 1/4 inch (58mm) or 3 1/8 inch (80mm) width paper. Not sure which one fits your printer?

  • Check your current roll: Look at the width printed on the cardboard core
  • Check your printer model: Search "[your printer model] paper size"
  • Common sizes: Square uses 3 1/8 inch, Clover uses 3 1/8 inch, most credit card terminals use 2 1/4"
  • Call us: (888) 881-6834 — we'll help you identify the right size

Phenol-Free vs Standard Thermal Paper

What's the difference?

  • Standard thermal paper: May contain BPA or BPS for image development
  • Phenol-free paper: Uses alternative developers, no BPA, BPS, or phenols
  • Health-conscious choice: Safer for employees handling receipts daily
  • Environmental: Clarion brand is certified recyclable in North America
  • Performance: Identical print quality and 10-year image life

Many businesses are switching to phenol-free for employee wellness programs.
