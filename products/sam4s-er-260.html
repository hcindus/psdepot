Entry-Level Professional Cash Management

The SAM4S ER-260 is a reliable electronic cash register designed for small to medium businesses. With its raised keyboard and POP-up customer display, it delivers the functionality you need at an affordable price point.

Key Features

  • 2,000 PLU Capacity: Handle your product catalog with ease
  • 20 Departments: Organize sales by category for detailed reporting
  • Raised Keyboard: Tactile feedback with individual mechanical keys for fast, accurate entry
  • Dual Receipt Printer: Journal + receipt printer with auto-cutter
  • POP-up Customer Display: Clear visibility with item name and price
  • Multiple Payment Options: Cash, check, credit, and multiple tender types

Ideal For

  • Small restaurants and cafes
  • Retail stores
  • Convenience stores
  • Quick-service establishments
  • Pop-up shops and seasonal businesses

Why Choose the ER-260?

Unlike cloud-based POS systems with recurring monthly fees, the ER-260 is a one-time purchase. Program it once, and it runs independently — no internet required, no subscription costs, no surprises. Perfect for businesses that need reliable, offline-capable transaction processing at an entry-level price.
