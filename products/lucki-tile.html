Product Tile v1.0 Specification

Standardized component structure for all product tiles across psdepot.com

Required Elements:

  • Image container: 4:3 aspect ratio, gradient background fallback
  • Stock badge: Green for in-stock, orange for low stock
  • Category tag: Positioned top-right
  • SKU: Small text, uppercase
  • Title: 2-line max with ellipsis
  • Price: Large, accent color
  • CTA buttons: Primary (View) + Secondary (Quote)

Design Tokens:

  • Primary: #0A1A2F (AGI Deep Tech Blue)
  • Accent: #FF7A00 (Performance Orange)
  • Success: #48bb78 (In Stock)
  • Border radius: 12px
  • Shadow on hover: 0 8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.12)
  • Max width: 320px
