The OrionStar Lucki is an innovative AI-powered delivery robot designed to transform customer service in hospitality and food service environments. Equipped with advanced LiDAR sensors and Intel RealSense depth cameras, Lucki navigates complex spaces with precision—detecting obstacles, planning optimal routes, and delivering orders safely to your guests.

🎯 Perfect For

Restaurants seeking to enhance service during peak hours, hotels looking to offer unique guest experiences, cafes wanting to stand out from competitors, and event venues requiring efficient catering solutions.

🧠 Intelligent Interaction

Lucki's 10.1-inch touchscreen display provides interactive customer engagement while its 6-microphone array with 360° sound source positioning enables natural voice interactions. With support for 31 languages, Lucki serves diverse customer bases with ease.

🔋 Built for Business
