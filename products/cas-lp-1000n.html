The Best Scale in Its Class

The CAS LP-1000N is the best scale on the market today in its class. A feature-rich, easy-to-use label printing scale designed for delis, convenience stores, grocery stores, and supermarkets. With its ability to use the most popular label formats, the LP-1000N delivers professional labeling and weighing performance at an affordable price point.

Key Features

  • 30 x 0.01 lb Capacity: Precision weighing for accurate pricing every time
  • 4,000 PLU Storage: Store up to 4,000 price look-up codes for quick access
  • 30 Standard Label Formats: Choose from popular label layouts including standard scale labels
  • 54 Preset Keys: Quick-access keys for your most popular items
  • Thermal Printing: No ink ribbons needed — clean, quiet operation
  • Auto-Prepack Feature: Streamline your packaging workflow

Advanced Printing Capabilities

  • Ingredients Printing: Up to 400 characters per PLU (8 lines x 50 characters)
  • Commodity Name: Up to 56 characters per PLU for clear product identification
  • Date Printing: Packed-on date and sell-by date on every label
  • Label Sizes: Supports 58 x 30mm to 58 x 100mm labels
  • Connectivity: Standard RS-232C and USB ports included

NTEP Certified & Legal for Trade

  • NTEP Certification: Certificate #92-169 — legal for commercial use
  • Measurement Canada Approved: AM-5590 certification for Canadian businesses
  • Legal for Trade: Approved for pricing items sold by weight

  • Supermarkets and grocery stores
  • Convenience stores
  • Meat and seafood markets
  • Bakeries
