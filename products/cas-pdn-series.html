About the CAS PDN Series

Say goodbye to complicated weighing processes and hello to seamless transactions with the CAS PDN POS Interface Scale. The CAS PDN scale works together with your Point of Sale (POS) system or ECR. Product weights are transferred directly into the transaction. No manual entry, no delays – just a swift and accurate sale.

Designed to elevate your customer's retail experience, the cutting-edge PDN POS Interface Scale combines precision with user-friendliness, making every sale a breeze. The bright and easy-to-read display ensures that both operators and customers can easily view the weight of the product being purchased.

Key Features

  • USB Powered: The PDN is powered through the USB port. No need for extra cables in your point-of-sale set up. Simply connect the USB cable to a POS terminal's USB port.
  • Multiple Interface Options: USB standard with optional Bluetooth and RS-232C connectivity available.
  • Optional Remote Display: 94 x 28 mm 6-digit LED remote display with X' of cable for customer viewing.
  • Edge Tray Design: Recessed applications supported with edge tray option.
