Professional-Grade Cash Management

The SAM4S ER-940 is a high-capacity electronic cash register designed for demanding retail and food service environments. With its flat, spill-resistant keyboard and raised customer display, it delivers the durability and visibility your business needs.

Key Features

  • 15,000 PLU Capacity: Handle extensive product catalogs with ease
  • 99 Departments: Organize sales by category for detailed reporting
  • Flat Keyboard Design: Easy to clean, ideal for food service environments
  • Dual Receipt Printer: Journal + receipt printer with auto-cutter
  • Raised Customer Display: Clear visibility with item name and price
  • Multiple Payment Options: Cash, check, credit, and multiple tender types

Ideal For

  • Restaurants and cafes
  • Retail stores with high SKU counts
  • Convenience stores
  • Quick-service establishments

Why Choose the ER-940?

Unlike cloud-based POS systems with recurring monthly fees, the ER-940 is a one-time purchase. Program it once, and it runs independently — no internet required, no subscription costs, no surprises. Perfect for businesses that need reliable, offline-capable transaction processing.
