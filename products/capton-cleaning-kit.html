Product Description

The Capton Pourer Cleaning Kit provides everything you need to keep your beverage portion control pourers clean and functioning at peak performance. Regular cleaning prevents buildup, ensures accurate portions, and extends the life of your pourers.

This complete kit includes specialized brushes designed to clean the internal mechanisms of Capton pourers without damaging the precision components, along with a cleaning solution formulated for food-contact equipment.

Kit Contents:

  • 3x Nylon cleaning brushes (various sizes)
  • 8 oz bottle of pourer cleaning solution
  • Storage case with compartments
  • Cleaning instructions and maintenance guide
  • Microfiber cleaning cloth

Recommended Use: Clean pourers weekly in high-volume environments, or bi-weekly for moderate use. Proper maintenance ensures accurate portions and extends equipment life.

Compatible with: All Capton bottle top pourers (1 oz, 1.5 oz, 2 oz, and wine pourers).
