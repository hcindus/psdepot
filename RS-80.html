A Native Android POS That Runs Offline

The Reggie Starr RS-80 is a purpose-built Android point-of-sale application engineered for restaurants, retail, and food service. Built natively in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose, it delivers a fast, tactile checkout experience without the recurring fees of cloud-based systems.

Key Capabilities

  • Calculator Keypad: 7-8-9 top layout for fast, familiar numeric entry
  • Flexible Tax Engine: Exclusive, inclusive, and per-item exempt rates
  • Open Price: Zero-PLU triggers custom price entry on the fly
  • ESC/POS Printing: Thermal receipt output over USB and Bluetooth
  • Offline-First Storage: Encrypted local database — no cloud dependency

Ideal For

  • Quick-service restaurants and cafes
  • Retail and convenience stores
  • Food trucks and mobile vendors
  • Businesses requiring offline, data-local POS
