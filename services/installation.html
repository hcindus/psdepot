Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical POS installation take? +
Most installations take 2-4 hours for a single terminal setup. Complex multi-location installs or custom configurations may take longer. We pre-configure as much as possible off-site to minimize your downtime.
Do you install POS systems on weekends? +
Yes, we offer weekend and evening installations to minimize disruption to your business. Weekend appointments book up quickly, so schedule in advance.
What if I need help after installation? +
All installations include 30 days of phone support. We also offer ongoing support contracts and on-site service calls at $180/hour. Remote troubleshooting is available for many issues.
Can you integrate with my existing accounting software? +
Yes, we have experience integrating with QuickBooks, Xero, and other popular accounting packages. Let us know your software during consultation and we'll plan the integration.
Do you provide training for my staff? +
Yes, every installation includes hands-on staff training. We cover daily operations, refunds, discounts, reporting, and basic troubleshooting. We stay until your team feels confident.
