Self-Checkout, Done Right

Self-checkout is about more than scanners and screens. When it's done right, it moves small, quick transactions out of the staffed lanes — cutting wait times on both sides. When it's done poorly, it's a source of frustration, maintenance calls, and loss.

We build self-checkout around your existing POS and your real floor traffic. We configure hardware, tune the attend-express model to your store, apply loss-prevention controls, and keep it all running with the same support you trust for every other lane.

Throughput & ROI

The goal is simple: shorter lines and a staff that's where customers need them. Self-checkout shines on small orders — a hand basket, a couple of items — where a full staffed transaction is overkill.

Your return comes from moving those transactions off the staffed lanes, freeing cashiers for service, department help, or stocking. Combined with the low cost of self-checkout consumables and reliable hardware, the model works best for stores with steady small-basket volume.

We'll assess your traffic mix and lane demands, then give you an honest read on whether (and where) self-checkout earns its place on your floor.
