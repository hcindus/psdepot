Full Feature Breakdown

SAM4POS goes beyond a standard cash register — touchscreen sales, programming tools, reporting, device setup, and payment features in one platform.

Touchscreen Sales & Screen Editing

Editable menu screens, button layouts, item groups, and quick keys organized around how your team rings sales. Modifiers for menu options, sizes, add-ons, and substitutions.

PLUs, Items & Inventory

Price look-ups for menu items, retail products, barcode items, open-price and preset-price items. Inventory-style functions, price levels, and label printing for scan-based retail.

Kitchen Printing & Kitchen Video

Route orders to kitchen printers by preparation area. Kitchen video (kitchen display) can be configured to display orders for food-service prep.

Barcode Scanning & Scales

UPC scanner support for convenience, liquor, grocery, and retail. Scale integration for deli, produce, candy, and weighed-item sales.

Payments & Tenders

Cash, credit, debit, check, gift card, and other tenders programmable through tender keys. Integrated payment and gift-card readiness with EMV-capable setup options.

Tax Programming

Tax rates and tax groups for restaurants, retail, grocery, liquor, and mixed-product businesses, including taxable vs. non-taxable item handling.

Employees & Security

Employees, job roles, authority levels, and access controls for discounts, voids, refunds, reports, and manager-level operations.

Reporting & End-of-Day

Sales, item, employee, tender, tax, and group reports, plus end-of-day closing with cash declaration and report review.

Receipts & Device Setup

Customer receipts with messages and logos, plus receipt printers, customer displays, cash drawers, coin dispensers, and other supported peripherals.

Multi-Terminal Operation

Plan stations, network settings, terminal roles, device mapping, printers, and cash drawers for multi-terminal setups.

SAM4POS FAQ

Real answers to the questions we hear most from curious operators.

What is SAM4POS?

SAM4POS is SAM4S point-of-sale software used on supported SAM4S POS terminals. It gives merchants touchscreen sales, programming tools, reporting, device setup, and payment-related features that go beyond a standard electronic cash register — while staying in the SAM4S family.

What businesses is SAM4POS good for?

Restaurants, bars, quick-service food businesses, convenience stores, grocery stores, liquor stores, retail shops, cafeterias, delis, and specialty merchants that need more flexibility than a basic cash register.

What hardware runs SAM4POS?

SAM4POS runs on supported SAM4S POS terminals and compatible setups, including the Android-based SAP-series terminals we sell and service. Contact us and we'll confirm the right terminal for your configuration.

Is SAM4POS the same as a cash register PC utility?

No. SAM4POS is a point-of-sale software platform for daily checkout and business operations. A PC utility is a separate program used to communicate with and program a compatible electronic cash register.

Does SAM4POS work with integrated payments?

SAM4POS is electronic payment and gift card processing ready. Integrated setup depends on your processor, payment device, SAM4POS version, programmed tender keys, and supported configuration — we'll help match one to your store.

Can SAM4POS route orders to the kitchen?

Yes. Kitchen printing and kitchen video can be configured for restaurants, bars, delis, cafes, and quick-service food businesses, routing items to preparation areas based on your setup.

Will you help with setup and support?

Absolutely. We provide pre-configured software installation, training, and ongoing support. We've been servicing SAM4S equipment since 2005 — call (888) 881-6834 for help.
