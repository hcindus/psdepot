Why Coffee Shops Need the Right POS Supplies

Coffee shops face a unique set of challenges: limited counter space, high transaction volume in short bursts, and often a mix of Square or Clover tablet POS systems alongside traditional registers. Your POS supplies need to match that reality — compact where space matters, durable where volume demands it, and compatible with the evolving mix of hardware on your counter.

Small Space, Smart Paper Choices

Most independent coffee shops run on Square, Clover, or Toast POS systems that use tablet-based interfaces with compact thermal receipt printers. The 2 1/4" x 74' credit card paper (SKU 15-741) is the workhorse of the cafe world — it fits nearly every mobile POS printer and handheld payment terminal. For shops that also run a full countertop printer for kitchen or order tickets, the 3 1/8" x 230' thermal rolls (SKU 54-230) keep your back-of-house running without constant reloads. Check our receipt paper sizes guide if you're unsure which size your hardware takes.

Beverage Consistency with Capton Pourers

If your coffee shop has expanded into beer, wine, or craft cocktails, consistency is brand identity. A latte tastes the same every time because your recipe is measured — your bar pours should be, too. The Capton 1 oz pourer gives you exact measurements for syrups, bitters, and spirits, while the 1.5 oz pourer handles standard cocktail builds. For shops dipping into wine service, the Capton 5 oz wine pourer delivers consistent glass pours every time.

Cash Handling for the Counter Rush

Even in a largely cashless world, plenty of coffee shops still handle significant cash volume — especially in neighborhoods where tips and small purchases dominate. The 72-100 cash drawer is compact enough for tight counter setups yet durable enough for hundreds of daily opens. Pair it with a SAM4S ER-260 cash register for a complete counter system that won't break the bank.

Frequently Asked Questions

What receipt paper does Square register use?

Square's thermal receipt printers use standard 2 1/4" (57mm) or 3 1/8" (80mm) rolls depending on the model. The Square Terminal uses 2 1/4" rolls. Our SKU 15-741 is a perfect fit. For Square Register with the customer-facing display printer, 3 1/8" rolls are standard — see our SKU 54-230.

How often should I reorder receipt paper for a busy cafe?

A busy cafe processing 300-500 transactions daily will go through roughly one case of thermal paper per month. We recommend keeping a two-month supply on hand — especially before holiday and summer peak seasons. Our bulk ordering guide covers recommended inventory levels by business type and volume.
