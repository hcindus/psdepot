Why Hotels Need Consistent POS Supplies Across Properties

Hotels and motels operate POS systems across multiple departments — the front desk, gift shop, restaurant, bar, and sometimes the spa or fitness center. Each terminal prints receipts, guest folios, and transaction records. When one printer goes down because someone forgot to reorder paper, it impacts the guest experience. Hospitality is about seamless service, and your POS supplies are invisible infrastructure that makes that possible.

Front Desk: Guest Folios & Payment Receipts

The front desk is the financial hub of any hotel. Whether you're printing guest folios at checkout, processing incidental charges, or printing payment receipts, your receipt printer needs to work every single time. Most hotel front desks use standard thermal receipt printers compatible with our 3 1/8" x 230' thermal rolls (SKU 54-230). For properties using separate card terminals, the 2 1/4" credit card paper (SKU 15-741) keeps those devices stocked.
