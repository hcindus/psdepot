Thermal vs. Bond Paper: What Retailers Should Know

Most modern retail POS systems use thermal printers — they're faster, quieter, and require no ribbon replacement. Our 3 1/8" thermal rolls (SKU 54-230) work with virtually every thermal POS printer on the market. However, some retailers — especially those printing duplicate receipts or gift receipts — still rely on impact printers with bond paper. Our 13-305 bond paper and ERC ribbons keep those legacy systems running. Not sure what your printer takes? Consult our thermal vs. bond paper guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know which receipt paper size my retail POS printer uses?

Check your printer's manual or measure an existing roll. Most retail thermal printers use 3 1/8" (80mm) rolls. Credit card terminals typically use 2 1/4" (57mm). Our receipt paper sizes guide covers all common widths and which printers map to each.

Can I use the same paper for gift receipts and standard receipts?

If your printer supports 2-ply carbonless paper, yes. Our 2-ply carbonless paper (SKU CC-235) produces a customer copy and a store copy in one pass — perfect for gift receipts and exchanges.
