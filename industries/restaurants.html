Why Restaurants Need Specialized POS Supplies

Restaurants operate in one of the most demanding environments for POS hardware and consumables. From grease-laden air in the kitchen to spilled drinks at the bar, your receipt paper, ribbons, and cash management tools take a beating every shift. Choosing the right supplies isn't just about cost — it's about reliability when your restaurant is slammed on a Friday night.

Thermal Paper That Survives the Kitchen

Kitchen printers sit feet from hot grills, fryers, and heat lamps. Standard thermal paper can blacken or become illegible when exposed to sustained heat. Our 3 1/8" x 230' thermal rolls (SKU 54-230) are engineered with higher heat-resistance thresholds, keeping your kitchen tickets readable from the expo station to the pass. For restaurants using impact printers for kitchen orders, our bond paper and ERC ribbons produce crisp duplicates that don't smear under humid kitchen conditions.

Pour Control = Pour Profits

The difference between a profitable bar and a break-even bar often comes down to pour accuracy. Over-pouring by just 1/8 oz per drink costs a busy restaurant thousands annually. The Capton 1.5 oz pourer delivers exact pours every time, and the Capton Variety Pack outfits every well with the right tool. Combine with the Capton Cleaning Kit for consistent accuracy across hundreds of shifts.
