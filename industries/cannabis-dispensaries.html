POS Supplies for Compliant Cannabis Retail

Cannabis dispensaries operate at the intersection of retail and regulated industry — every transaction must be documented to state track-and-trace standards. Your receipt paper, cash handling, and printing systems are part of your compliance infrastructure. Cutting corners on supplies is cutting corners on compliance.

Compliance-Grade Receipt Paper

State tracking systems like METRC and BioTrack require detailed receipts that include product codes, batch numbers, weights, and regulatory disclaimers — all of which must remain legible. Our 3 1/8" x 230' thermal rolls (SKU 54-230) print with high contrast and resolution, ensuring barcodes scan cleanly and text stays readable. For dispensaries positioning as wellness-focused, phenol-free thermal paper (SKU PF-230) eliminates BPA/BPS from customer touchpoints.

Cash-Intensive Operations

Despite banking reform progress, most dispensaries remain cash-heavy operations. The 5-Bill 5-Coin Cash Drawer (SKU 72-100) handles high-denomination transactions with five separate bill slots — keep $20s, $50s, and $100s organized and secure. Spring-loaded durability means it holds up to thousands of daily transactions.

Pair with the CAS LP-1000N label printer for on-demand product tags and bin labels that keep your back-of-house organized for state inspectors.

Frequently Asked Questions

What receipt requirements do cannabis dispensaries need to meet?

Requirements vary by state but typically include: dispensary license number, product name and weight, batch/lot number, date and time of sale, budtender ID, regulatory disclaimers, and total price including taxes. Our thermal receipt paper prints with the resolution needed for scannable barcodes and legible small-print compliance text.

How should dispensaries handle cash-heavy transactions?

Use a 5-Bill 5-Coin Cash Drawer with organized compartments for high-denomination bills. Implement regular cash drops throughout the day, use a smart safe if volume warrants it, and train budtenders on cash-handling procedures. Our cash drawers support the frequent open/close cycles of a busy dispensary.
