POS Supplies That Survive the Wash Bay

Car washes and detailing shops are among the toughest environments for POS equipment. Water spray, cleaning chemicals, humidity, and constant foot traffic from wet customers all conspire against your receipt paper and hardware. You need supplies that don't fail when they get damp.

Moisture-Resistant Receipt Paper

Standard thermal paper curls and fades when exposed to humidity. Our 3 1/8" x 230' thermal rolls (SKU 54-230) feature enhanced coating technology that provides better resistance to ambient moisture. For detailing shops where chemicals are airborne, our phenol-free thermal paper (SKU PF-230) adds the benefit of being BPA-free while maintaining moisture resistance.

Rugged Cash Handling

Car washes handle a lot of cash — from express exterior washes to full-detail packages that run hundreds of dollars. The 5-Bill 5-Coin Cash Drawer (SKU 72-100) features sealed internal mechanisms that resist dust and moisture infiltration, making it ideal for garage-adjacent offices and kiosk setups.

Mobile & Bay-Side Payments

Full-service car washes and detailers often process payment at the vehicle — not at a front counter. Our 2 1/4" credit card paper (SKU 15-741) fits handheld POS terminals so your team can swipe cards and print receipts right at the bay. Faster service, better tips, and no walk-back to the register.

Multi-Service Tracking

Car washes typically offer tiered packages (basic, deluxe, premium) plus à la carte add-ons (wax, undercarriage, interior detail). The SAM4S ER-260 cash register with programmable department keys lets you track each service tier and add-on separately — essential for understanding which packages drive your revenue. Frequently Asked Questions

Does humidity really affect thermal receipt paper?

Yes — thermal paper is sensitive to moisture and humidity. In car wash environments, standard paper can curl, fade, or become unreadable within hours. Our SKU 54-230 rolls use enhanced moisture-resistant coating. Store spare rolls in sealed containers and keep printers away from direct spray zones.

What's the best cash drawer for a car wash kiosk?

The 5-Bill 5-Coin Cash Drawer (SKU 72-100) — its sealed mechanism and spring-loaded durability make it ideal for environments with dust, moisture, and chemical exposure. For unattended payment kiosks, consider integrating with a thermal printer using compact receipt paper.
