POS Supplies for Event Professionals

Catering and event services are logistics operations wrapped in hospitality. You manage deposits, final payments, bar tabs, per-head counts, and last-minute add-ons — often at a folding table in a venue you've never worked before. Your POS kit needs to be portable, complete, and foolproof.

The Mobile POS Kit

Every caterer needs an event-ready POS kit. Core components: a tablet running your POS app, a mobile thermal printer loaded with 2 1/4" credit card paper (SKU 15-741) for on-the-spot receipts, a 5-Bill 5-Coin Cash Drawer (SKU 72-100) for bar sales and cash payments, and spare 3 1/8" thermal rolls (SKU 54-230) for venue-installed printers. Pack it in a Pelican case and you're ready for any venue.

Deposit & Final Balance Receipts

Catering transactions span weeks or months — deposit at booking, progress payments during planning, final balance after the event. Every payment needs a clean, professional receipt. Our thermal paper produces sharp prints that represent your brand well and stay legible through the entire client relationship.

Event Bar Pour Control

Open bars at events are margin-killers without portion control. The Capton Variety Pack (SKU CAP-VARY-12PK) outfits your event bar with 1 oz, 1.5 oz, 2 oz, and 5 oz wine pourers — every drink poured to spec. For wine service at plated dinners, the Capton Wine Pourer (SKU CAP-WINE-5OZ) ensures every table gets the same pour. For tasting stations and flights, the 1 oz Capton pourer is perfect for sample portions.

Multi-Venue Consistency

If you cater across multiple venues, standardized supplies keep your operations running smoothly. Same paper in every printer, same pourers at every bar, same cash drawer at every checkout. Our bulk program lets large catering companies standardize across all event teams with volume pricing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should go in a catering POS event kit?

Essentials: tablet with POS app, mobile thermal printer, spare 2 1/4" and 3 1/8" paper rolls, cash drawer with starting bank, card reader, extension cord, power strip, and backup battery. Add Capton pourers if you're running a bar. Keep it all in a waterproof hard case — venues are unpredictable.

How do I handle event bar profitability?

Pour control is the single biggest lever. The Capton Variety Pack eliminates over-pouring and typically pays for itself within 2-3 events. For open bars, track bottle counts before and after — the difference with Capton pourers is measurable and dramatic.
