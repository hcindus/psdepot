Why Auto Repair Shops Need Durable POS Consumables

Auto repair shops and dealerships operate in environments that are tough on office equipment. Grease, oil, dust, and temperature swings are part of daily life — and your POS supplies need to hold up. A service invoice that smears from moisture on a mechanic's hands isn't just unprofessional — it can create disputes about what work was authorized. Quality POS consumables are an investment in customer trust.

Service Invoices: The Most Important Document You Print

In the automotive service business, the repair order or service invoice is a legal document. It details what was authorized, what was performed, what parts were used, and what was charged. Many shops still use impact printers for service invoices because they produce durable, multi-part carbon copies — one for the customer, one for the shop file, and one for the technician. Our 2-ply carbonless paper (SKU CC-235) paired with ERC 30/34/38 ribbons (SKU 62245) delivers clean, legible duplicates that won't fade or smear. For shops using thermal printers for customer-facing receipts, our 3 1/8" thermal paper (SKU 54-230) provides crisp, professional print quality.

Parts Counter POS

Dealership parts departments and independent parts counters process a high volume of transactions — walk-in retail customers, wholesale accounts, and internal service department transfers. The right POS paper matters. The 13-305 bond paper works with Epson and Epson-compatible impact printers common in parts departments, while the 54-230 thermal rolls serve the countertop POS terminals increasingly adopted by modern parts operations. Cash Handling in the Shop

Many independent repair shops still handle significant cash and check payments — especially for smaller jobs and retail parts sales. Frequently Asked Questions

What's the best paper for automotive service invoices?

For shops still using impact printers, 2-ply carbonless paper (SKU CC-235) with ERC ribbons produces durable multi-part copies — ideal for customer/shop copies. For modern thermal printers, our 54-230 thermal rolls provide excellent single-copy quality.

How do I know which ribbon cartridge my shop's impact printer uses?

Most automotive service printers use ERC 30, 34, or 38 series ribbons. Our SKU 62245 ribbons are compatible with all three series. Check your printer model against our printer compatibility guide for confirmation.
