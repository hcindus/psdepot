POS Supplies for 24/7 Operations

Gas stations and convenience stores never close — and neither should your POS system. Your supplies need to handle graveyard shift reliability, high-volume pump printer output, and the constant cash handling that comes with fuel, snacks, and everything in between.

Pump Printer Paper

Fuel dispenser receipt printers burn through paper fast — every fill-up generates a receipt, and many customers grab receipts even for card transactions. Our 2 1/4" x 74' credit card paper (SKU 15-741) fits most Gilbarco, Wayne, and Tokheim dispenser printers. For counter terminals, stock up on 3 1/8" x 230' thermal rolls (SKU 54-230) in bulk — multi-pump stations can go through 10+ rolls per week.

Cash Management for C-Stores

Gas stations handle more cash than most retail businesses — fuel is still frequently paid in cash, and c-store purchases add to the drawer. The 5-Bill 5-Coin Cash Drawer (SKU 72-100) provides organized, secure cash storage with spring-loaded durability for constant use. For multi-register stations, we offer volume pricing through our bulk program.

C-Store Food Programs

Modern c-stores are food destinations — roller grills, deli counters, grab-and-go meals. The CAS LP-1000N label printer handles shelf tags, date labels, and price stickers for fresh food items. Pair it with a SAM4S SAP-630 register for department-level tracking that separates fuel from food from merchandise.

Inventory & Age Verification

Alcohol, tobacco, and lottery sales add complexity to every transaction. Your receipts need to print clearly with all required information — item codes, regulatory numbers, age verification flags. Our thermal paper produces sharp, detailed prints that stand up to audit requirements and customer scrutiny alike.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should gas stations change pump receipt paper?

High-volume stations may need to change pump receipt rolls every 1-3 days depending on traffic. Stock 2-3 cases of 3 1/8" thermal rolls and 2 1/4" credit card paper per pump bank. Our bulk pricing makes it cost-effective to keep a month's supply on hand.

What printers do most gas station pumps use?

Most fuel dispensers use 2 1/4" or 3 1/8" thermal receipt printers. Check your dispenser model — Gilbarco, Wayne, and Tokheim each have specific paper requirements. Our printer compatibility guide covers all major pump manufacturers.
