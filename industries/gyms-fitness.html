Frequently Asked Questions

What POS setup works best for a gym front desk?

A standard gym setup includes a POS terminal, thermal receipt printer, barcode scanner for retail items, and a cash drawer. Our 5-Bill 5-Coin Cash Drawer paired with 3 1/8" thermal rolls covers most fitness businesses. For studios with smoothie bars, add a second printer station dedicated to food service.

Is BPA-free receipt paper worthwhile for gyms?

Absolutely. Your members are health-conscious — that's why they're at your gym. Handing them a BPA-laden receipt undercuts your wellness positioning. Our phenol-free thermal paper aligns your POS experience with your brand values at a marginal cost increase.
