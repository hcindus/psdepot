Why Bakeries Need Dedicated POS Supplies

Bakery counters are unique retail environments. Flour dust floats through the air. Hands move from dough to register and back. Custom orders come in handwritten, production sheets go out printed. Your POS supplies need to work clean in a messy environment and print legibly for orders that must be exact.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does flour dust affect POS printers?

Yes — flour dust is abrasive and can accelerate wear on thermal print heads and impact printer mechanisms. Clean your printer heads weekly with isopropyl alcohol and keep printers covered overnight. Our ERC ribbons are designed for dusty kitchen environments with reinforced spools that resist particle buildup.

What's the best receipt paper for bakeries with open kitchens?

BPA-free thermal paper is the smart choice for open-concept bakeries where customers can see the kitchen. Our phenol-free thermal paper (SKU PF-230) eliminates chemical concerns and prints with the same quality as standard thermal rolls.
