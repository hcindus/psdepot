Why Salons & Spas Need Quality POS Consumables

Salons and spas operate on appointments, relationships, and trust. When a client pays for a $200 color service or a $350 spa package, the receipt they receive is part of the experience. A faded, poorly printed receipt doesn't match the premium service you just delivered. Your POS consumables should reflect the same quality standard as everything else in your business.

Compact POS Systems, Compact Paper

Most independent salons and spas use tablet-based POS systems — Square, Clover, Vagaro, Boulevard, or similar platforms. These systems typically pair with compact thermal printers or handheld card terminals that use 2 1/4" (57mm) receipt paper. Our credit card paper (SKU 15-741) is the right size for virtually every salon POS setup. For salons that run a full-size countertop printer for detailed service receipts or gift card transactions, the 3 1/8" thermal rolls (SKU 54-230) provide professional-quality output.

Eco-Conscious Choices for Wellness Brands

Many salons and spas market themselves on natural, organic, and eco-friendly values. If your brand makes sustainability promises to clients, your POS supplies should align. Our PF-230 phenol-free thermal paper delivers the same reliable performance as standard thermal paper without BPA or BPS — a detail that resonates with the wellness-conscious clientele many salons serve.

Retail + Service = Flexible POS Needs

Most salons sell retail products alongside services — shampoo, conditioner, styling products, skincare. Your POS needs to handle both service transactions and retail sales seamlessly. The SAM4S ER-260 cash register supports department keys for separate tracking of services, retail, and gift cards — giving you clear revenue visibility without expensive salon management software. Frequently Asked Questions

What receipt paper does Square work with for salon use?

Square's receipt printers are compatible with standard 2 1/4" (57mm) and 3 1/8" (80mm) thermal paper rolls depending on the model. Our SKU 15-741 fits Square Terminal and most compact printers. For Square Register, use SKU 54-230. Check our printer compatibility guide for specifics.

How do I choose between thermal and bond paper for my salon?

If your printer uses heat to print (no ribbon), it's thermal — use our thermal paper. If it uses a ribbon cartridge, it's an impact printer — use our bond paper. Most modern salon POS systems use thermal printers. Our thermal vs. bond paper guide explains the differences in detail.
