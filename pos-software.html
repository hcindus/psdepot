What is SAM4POS?

A full point-of-sale software platform from SAM4S — the hardware maker trusted by businesses in 100+ countries — engineered to turn capable SAM4S terminals into complete checkout and operations systems.

More than a cash register

SAM4POS is point-of-sale software used on supported SAM4S terminals and compatible setups. It gives merchants touchscreen sales, programming tools, reporting, device setup, and payment-related features that go beyond a standard electronic cash register.

You stay in the SAM4S family — the hardware brand behind your registers and terminals — while gaining the flexibility of a modern, screen-driven POS system.

Built for real operations

Because it's configurable by an authorized SAM4S dealer, SAM4POS is tailored to how your business actually rings sales — from a quick-serve counter to a full restaurant floor to a scan-heavy retail aisle.

  • Restaurants, bars & quick-service
  • Convenience, liquor & grocery stores
  • Retail, deli & specialty shops

Key Features

Verified capabilities you can build your operation around — every one supported by SAM4POS configuration.

🖥️

Touchscreen Checkout

Screen-based sales with editable menu screens, button layouts, item groups, and quick keys organized around how your team rings sales.

🍔

Restaurant Kitchen Routing

Menu screens, modifiers, and gratuity for food service — plus kitchen printer routing and kitchen video (kitchen display) for order preparation areas.

📦

PLU, Inventory & Labels

Price look-ups (PLUs), item groups, inventory-style functions, price levels, and shelf/barcode label printing for retail, grocery, and deli applications.

🔔

Barcode Scanning & Scales

UPC scanner support for scan-based businesses and scale integration for grocery, deli, produce, and weighed-item sales.

💳

Integrated Payments

Electronic payment and gift card processing ready — cash, credit, debit, gift card, and more through tender keys and optional integrated payment devices.

📊

Reporting & Back-Office

Sales, item, employee, tender, tax, and group reports — plus end-of-day closing procedures and options for remote report access depending on setup.

👥

Employees & Security

Employees, job roles, authority levels, and access controls so discounts, voids, refunds, reports, and manager functions stay controlled.

🧮

Tax Handling

Flexible tax rates and tax groups for restaurants, retail, grocery, liquor, and mixed-product businesses — including taxable and non-taxable item rules.

🖨️

Receipts & Devices

Customer receipts with messages and logos, plus receipt printers, customer displays, cash drawers, coin dispensers, and other supported peripherals.

Cloud & Back-Office Benefits

SAM4POS brings your checkout data and operations together — with the reliability of dedicated SAM4S hardware.

Smarter data, fewer blind spots

  • Detailed reporting: sales, items, employees, tenders, taxes, and groups — not just a closing total.
  • Remote access options: depending on installed configuration and licensing, reports can be reviewed away from the terminal.
  • Multi-terminal operation: plan stations, terminal roles, device mapping, and printer/cash-drawer assignment across a network.

Engineered to keep checkout moving
